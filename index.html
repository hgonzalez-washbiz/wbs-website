How we engage

A clear path from first call to monthly rhythm

We keep the process simple and honest. If we are not the right fit, we will say so quickly — so you do not waste time or budget.

  1. 1

    Intro call

    A short conversation about your goals, team, and where finance feels stuck. No pitch deck required.

  2. 2

    Current-state review

    We look at your books, systems, and reporting cadence so recommendations match reality — not a template.

  3. 3

    Proposal

    A custom scope and fee structure after that intro. You see what we will own month to month before you commit.

  4. 4

    Monthly rhythm

    Close, cash reporting, forecasts, and advisory touchpoints on a cadence that fits your business — with tax and audit prep support when those cycles hit.

How engagements are typically structured

Most clients work with us on a monthly retainer sized to scope. Cleanups, models, and discrete projects are often fixed-fee. Rates are not published online — after a short intro we send a custom proposal tailored to what you need.

About WBS

A small, selective firm invested in client success

Washington Business Solutions, LLC is a Maryland-based firm providing outsourced CFO and advisory services alongside full-suite accounting for small and mid-sized companies across Virginia (VA), Maryland (MD), and Washington, DC.

Unlike large national firms, we keep our client roster intentional. That means senior attention, a close working relationship, and a partner who is genuinely invested in how your business performs — not a rotating cast of junior staff. Clients can work with our team in English or Spanish.

Our message is simple: financial solutions with expertise and directness. We are the “where is my money” CFO — working on your behalf so leadership always has a clear, actionable view of cash, performance, and what comes next.
